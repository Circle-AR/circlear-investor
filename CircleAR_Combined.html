The Platform
Storytelling App
QR scan opens audio narration in any phone browser. No app download. Keeper's voice. Multilingual. Children's badge trails. Conservation giving.
Circle AR Tag
Wearable GPS via LoRa radio. No WiFi. No cellular. 15km range. 30-sec ping. SOS button. Auto audio for blind visitors. R15/day in ticket price.
Ranger Dashboard
Live web interface. Every visitor tracked. Geofencing. SOS alerts. One ranger covers the entire venue.
The Problem
Every zoo, national park, and heritage site has the same two problems. Visitors leave without emotional connection — and without spending. And 253 million blind and visually impaired people arrive to find nothing designed for them. Circle AR solves both in one platform.
